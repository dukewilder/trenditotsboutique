Our story

Trendi Tots Boutique is an online shop for toddler girls. Our dresses have full skirts, puffed sleeves and collars covered in embroidery.

Parents come to us before Easter, Christmas, birthdays and picture day. They want their daughter to look like the little girl in the family photo that hangs in the hallway for thirty years.

We choose every piece one by one, and we answer every message ourselves. If you are stuck between two sizes or two shades of pink, ask. Helping you pick is our favorite part of the job.

What we care about

  • More is more

    Between a plain dress and one with an extra ruffle, we stock the ruffle.

  • Made to be kept

    We choose pieces worth saving for a little sister or a memory box.

  • Sweet and well mannered

    Every message is warm and polite. We thank people and we fix mistakes fast.

  • Small shop service

    A real person answers, with a straight answer on sizing and shipping.
