Our story

Trendi Tots Boutique is an online shop for little girls. Our dresses have full skirts, puffed sleeves and collars covered in embroidery.

Parents come to us before Easter, Christmas, birthdays and picture day. They want their daughter to look like the little girl in the family photo that hangs in the hallway for thirty years.

Each dress is custom made, and we answer every message ourselves. If you are stuck between two sizes or two shades of pink, ask. Helping you pick is our favorite part of the job.

What we care about

  • More is more

    Between a plain dress and one with an extra ruffle, we make the ruffle.

  • Made for her

    Each dress is custom made and worth saving for a little sister or a memory box.

  • Sweet and well mannered

    Every message is warm and polite. We thank people and we fix mistakes fast.

  • Small shop service

    A real person answers, with a straight answer on sizing and shipping.

Ordering information

Made to order

Each dress is custom made after you order. Please allow three weeks for it to ship.

Personalization

Every dress page has a box for the name, number or wording you would like embroidered. Please check the spelling before you check out.

Sizing

Sizes run from 12 months to 5T. Use the size chart above, and choose the larger size when she is between two.

Shipping

Orders ship three weeks after they are placed. You will get an email with tracking when yours is on its way.
