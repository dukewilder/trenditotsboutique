PRINT NOTES

Every PDF in this folder is vector artwork at the real print size, with 0.125 inch of extra background (bleed) on each side.
The printer trims the bleed off. Upload the PDF and choose the size listed below.

Business card      3.5 x 2 in       The back carries the web address, trenditots.boutique.
Hang tag           2 x 3.5 in       The small circle marks where the hole goes.
Thank you card     4 x 6 in
Round stickers     2 in circle
Packaging label    4 x 2 in
Garment label      2 x 0.75 in      Woven label makers usually want one color. Send the cocoa wordmark from the Logos folder.
Gift certificate   7 x 3 in
Tissue and wrap    12 x 12 in       The pattern repeats, so the printer can tile it to any sheet size.

The files named "-preview.png" are pictures of the PDFs for quick viewing. Send the PDF to the printer.
Colors on paper come out slightly different from a screen. Ask for a printed proof on the first order.
